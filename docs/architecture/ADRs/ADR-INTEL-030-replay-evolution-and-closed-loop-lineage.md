# ADR-INTEL-030: Replay adds evidence and closed-loop history preserves intervention lineage
- Status: Accepted
- Phase: INTEL-P1
## Decision
Harness replay is optional and deferred from the historical-miner MVP. When
used, l9-harness executes replay through a public contract and returns new
evidence; Harness does not promote corpus candidates or rewrite historical
observations.
Historical reconstruction algorithms and schemas are versioned. Recomputed
claims are new derived claims, and admitted changes use existing correction or
retraction events.
When known, historical evidence records lineage for active defense packs,
rules, Resolver strategy sources, LSP interventions, and PR Repair
interventions.
## Consequences
Replay of one episode, duplicate delivery, cherry-picks of one patch, and
feedback generated by prior L9 rules are not independent evidence. Naturally
occurring history and L9-influenced outcomes remain distinguishable for later
recurrence and effectiveness analysis.
